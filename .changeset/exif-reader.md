`Exif`: reads EXIF metadata from JPEG, PNG, WebP, TIFF, HEIF, AVIF and JPEG XL files in plain TypeScript, with typed accessors for orientation, camera and lens, exposure, capture time and GPS location. It also edits tags, writes EXIF back into JPEG, PNG, WebP, JPEG XL, HEIF and AVIF files without re-encoding them, and strips EXIF and XMP entirely, or just the location (EXIF GPS and the location properties of XMP).
